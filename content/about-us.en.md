Genial Inc. is a small collective of students passionate about mathematics and computer science. Most of us are enrolled in the [Mathematics–Computer Science double degree (DLMI)](https://www.univ-jfc.fr/node/438) at the Institut national universitaire Champollion (INU Champollion), in France.

## What we do

We learn by building things. Rather than watching others do it, we jump into hands-on projects, often against the clock:

- **game jams** such as the Global Game Jam, where you have to make a complete game in 48 hours around a given theme;
- the **Nuit de l'Info**, a French national competition where student teams build a web application in a single night;
- and any other project that crosses our minds.

You will find what we have built on the [projects page]({{< relref "/projects" >}}).

## The Mathematics–Computer Science double degree

The DLMI at INU Champollion lets students work towards a bachelor's degree in mathematics and a bachelor's degree in computer science at the same time, in three years. The perfect playground for people who would rather not choose between the two.

To find out more, see the [official programme page](https://www.univ-jfc.fr/node/438) (in French).

## Get in touch

The easiest way to reach us is through our [GitHub organisation](https://github.com/Genial-Inc). If you like a challenge, you can also try our [contact form](/contact/), built during the Nuit de l'Info 2025: it doesn't send anything, but it will keep you busy for a while.
