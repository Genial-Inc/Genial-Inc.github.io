## A student collective from the DLMI

Genial Inc. is a student collective from the **Double Degree in Mathematics and Computer Science (DLMI)** at Institut national universitaire Champollion, in Albi, France.

The programme allows students to complete both bachelor's degrees in three years. It comprises 36 ECTS credits per semester and combines coursework from both disciplines.

We enjoy putting what we learn into practice through collaborative projects. Video games, programming and computer-science experiments are among our interests. Our topics vary, but we share an enthusiasm for building things, solving problems and working as a team.

## Our projects

We take part in student events and game jams, where we design and develop projects together under time constraints. This website presents some of our work, along with links to project pages and source repositories where available.
