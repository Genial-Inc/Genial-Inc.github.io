## About the project

Genial Bubble is our game for the Global Game Jam 2025, whose theme was "Bubble". Your only weapon: bubbles. Throw them to trap the spiders and snowmen standing in your way, or use them to bounce and trigger the mechanisms that will get you through the levels.

There are two kinds of bubbles:

- the **offensive bubble** traps any enemy it hits;
- the **puzzle bubble** stops wherever it lands: you can bounce on it or lodge it in a button to trigger a mechanism. Only one at a time, so choose carefully where you throw it!

## Features

- Retro gameplay inspired by arcade games.
- Built in 48 hours with Unity.
- Playable right in your browser.
- Coded with love by our crack team.

## Controls

The keys below are for a QWERTY keyboard; on an AZERTY keyboard, use Q and Z instead of A and W.

| Action | Key |
| --- | --- |
| Move | A / D |
| Jump | Space or W |
| Crouch | S |
| Throw an offensive bubble | J |
| Throw a puzzle bubble | H |
