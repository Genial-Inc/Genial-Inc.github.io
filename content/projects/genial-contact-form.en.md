## About the project

**Genial Contact Form** is a deliberately difficult contact form created for [Nuit de l'Info 2025](https://www.nuitdelinfo.com/).

The fields periodically move around, and the email address cannot be typed normally: each character must be launched with a slingshot. A bomb can even erase part of the address when things go wrong.

The result is a small interactive experiment that turns an ordinary contact form into a challenge.
