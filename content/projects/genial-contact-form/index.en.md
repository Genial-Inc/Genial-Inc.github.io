## About the project

Filling in a contact form is way too easy. So for the Nuit de l'Info 2025, we came up with the most annoying contact form we could think of.

The first name, phone and message fields work normally… but not the e-mail field. To type your address, you have to pick each character on a wheel, load it into a slingshot and **shoot** it into the right field. And that's not all:

- the fields **swap places** every ten seconds;
- the key stays still for one second after each shot;
- if you make a mistake, a **bomb** lets you wipe out what you've written;
- every shot comes with a sound, whether it hits or misses (turn the sound on, but not too loud);
- a timer measures how long you spent on the page, revealed when you finally send the form.

Good news: nothing is actually sent.
