## The pitch

Damn it! You've taken a Java test, and you want your results! But they still haven't given them yet…

You need to infiltrate the building, get the results from the teacher's computer, and get out of here ASAP! This is the perfect time to use your special mask (worth one trillion dollars), capable of imitating the DNA of anyone you touch. Use it wisely to make your way through the building without getting caught.

Be careful, however! If they get suspicious and catch you… you may never get to see the sunlight again.
