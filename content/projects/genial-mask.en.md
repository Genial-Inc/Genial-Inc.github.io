## About the project

You've just taken a Java test and want to know your result. There's one problem: your teacher still hasn't released the marks.

You'll have to infiltrate the building, access the teacher's computer and get out as quickly as possible. Fortunately, you have a special mask that can imitate the DNA of anyone you touch. Use it to move through the building without raising suspicion. Be careful: getting caught could mean never seeing daylight again.

The game was created for the [2026 Global Game Jam](https://globalgamejam.org/).
